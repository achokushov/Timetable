What is this site?

This site shows which rooms and teachers are free at BIS. It copies the school timetable automatically about every 30 minutes, so nothing here is typed in by hand. The time of the last update is in the top right corner.

  • Empty rooms: pick a day and a period (or several periods) to see which rooms are free.
  • Free teachers: the same for teachers.
  • Find a slot: pick several teachers, classes or rooms to see when all of them are free.
  • Best time: like Find a slot, but for “at least 4 of 5 are free”, for example for meetings.
  • Room usage: how busy each room is, and which times of the week are busiest.
  • Changes: what changed in the timetable since the previous update.
